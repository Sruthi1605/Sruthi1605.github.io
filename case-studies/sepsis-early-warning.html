Sepsis Early Warning System

A dual-expert Mixture of Experts model that flags sepsis risk early from multivariate time-series vitals and explains its reasoning with an LLM.

Featured · AI Research 2026 Blood Sciences / Healthcare ML
  • Python
  • PatchTST
  • Mixture of Experts
  • Time-Series Modeling
  • LLM

01. Overview

Sepsis is a life-threatening condition where the body's response to infection damages its own organs. Every hour of delayed treatment measurably lowers survival odds — so the goal was not "predict sepsis accurately" but "predict it early enough to act."

I designed a dual-expert Mixture of Experts (MoE) model that reads continuous patient vitals and flags early risk before clinical deterioration becomes obvious.

"Accuracy matters, but for sepsis the most important metric is lead time — hours saved when a model can beat routine observation."

02. The Problem

  • Sepsis signs are subtle early on and easy to miss in busy wards.
  • Patient data is multivariate time-series: vitals, labs, and demographics at irregular intervals.
  • Class imbalance — sepsis events are rare relative to normal patient-months.
  • Doctors didn't just need a risk score; they needed to understand why.

03. My Role & Approach

  • Worked on the time-series modelling component using PatchTST and PyTorch.
  • Prepared multivariate patient data for temporal learning and built the prediction pipeline.
  • Contributed to the dual-expert Mixture of Experts design that combines temporal patterns with clinical characteristics.
  • Integrated an explainable risk component: an LLM that generates interpretable explanations alongside each prediction for decision support.

04. Key Challenges

  • Irregular sampling: vitals arrive at uneven intervals — had to align and impute without losing signal.
  • Imbalance: boosted minority-class attention so early-risk frames weren't treated as noise.
  • Interpretability: an MoE is powerful but opaque; the LLM layer made the output actionable, not just a number.

05. Results & Impact

  • Early risk detection with severity classification across mild / moderate / severe.
  • Interpretable risk explanations for every prediction to support clinical decision-making.
  • A reusable time-series + LLM pipeline pattern I can adapt to other healthcare monitoring problems.
